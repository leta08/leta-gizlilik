LeTa IPTV Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy explains which information the LeTa IPTV app (iPhone, iPad, Apple TV and CarPlay; shown on the home screen as "LeTa Iptv") keeps on your device, which information leaves your device, where it goes and why. In this text the app is called "LeTa". It describes the App Store version; differences in the TestFlight test version are listed in their own section. The Turkish version is the primary text.

The app's interface is in Turkish. Settings paths are quoted as they appear in the app, with an English translation in brackets. You can also find a setting by typing its name into the search field at the top of Ayarlar (Settings).

In short

  • LeTa is a media player. It hosts and provides no content; channels and videos come from the sources you add.
  • You need no account to use LeTa or to buy LeTa Pro.
  • LeTa shows no ads. It contains no advertising tracking and no analytics or crash-reporting service. Diagnostic records stay on your device and leave it only if you share them.
  • Your sources, catalogue, profiles, favourites and watch history are kept on your device. If you use the optional LeTa account and Pro cloud sync, some records are stored on LeTa's server in the European Union (Ireland).
  • For film and series information, title names and content IDs are sent to TMDB. LeTa adds no account or device identifier to these requests.
  • LeTa Pro is bought through the App Store and verified with RevenueCat. Your payment details never reach LeTa or RevenueCat.

Data controller and contact

Data controller: LeTa Works, the developer of LeTa IPTV.

E-mail: contact@letaworks.com

Write to this address with any privacy question or request. For requests under Turkey's Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), see the "KVKK" section.

Information that stays on your device

  • Keychain: source passwords and tokens, sessions of services you connect (for example Trakt), your subtitle account's (OpenSubtitles) user name and password if “Bu cihazda bağlı kal” (Stay signed in on this device) is on, your LeTa account session, security keys and, if you saved it, the automatic backup password. These items are kept on this device only; they do not move to other devices with iCloud Keychain.
  • Encrypted library: the catalogue, profiles, favourites, watch history and your library edits are stored encrypted in the app's database. The audio and subtitle language (and track name) you pick for a series, channel or film is kept there too, with your profile; it is included in backups and nearby-device transfer and is not sent to the LeTa account. Playback measurements and crash records are also encrypted, with a separate key.
  • Profile PINs: not the PIN itself but a one-way hash of it is stored.
  • Other local storage: some preferences, browser bookmarks and sessions, profile photos and image caches.
  • Media files: downloads, recordings and rewind segments are playable files. They sit outside the encrypted database, in the app's own storage, and are not exported.
  • Widget and Top Shelf: the "İzlemeye Devam Et" (Continue Watching) widget on iPhone and iPad and the Top Shelf on Apple TV read the name, short details and picture of unfinished titles from a shared area on the device. Nothing is written there while a profile with a PIN or a locked profile is open. Apple TV loads Top Shelf pictures from the picture's own address.
  • Spotlight (iPhone and iPad): titles in your catalogue (name, short description, category) are added to the device's own Spotlight index so that they show up in search, and drop out after 30 days. Nothing is added for restricted or locked profiles or for the sample catalogue.

Deleting the app removes the files in its local storage. Keychain items, files you exported, copies on other devices and your accounts with connected services may be separate from that.

Information that leaves your device

Below, for each service: what is sent, why, and whether you can turn it off. Every server LeTa connects to can see the IP address a request comes from and what was requested; each service's own privacy policy applies.

Once you have added a source or turned on a service, LeTa does not ask again for every request. While features such as automatic refresh, preparing the neighbouring channel and loading artwork are on, requests can be made without you pressing play.

Sources you add

M3U playlists, Xtream Codes, Plex, Jellyfin, Emby, WebDAV and HTTP folders.

  • What is sent: the source address you entered; where needed, the user name and password or token; connection headers; which list, guide, picture or stream is requested. Jellyfin and Emby receive a random device identifier made for that source. When you connect Plex with a code, plex.tv receives the product name "LeTa" and a random identifier made for this installation; you confirm the code on Plex's own page.
  • Why: to load the channel list, the guide, artwork and the stream.
  • Your choice: you add the source and can delete it at any time. The source's provider decides how long it keeps information and how it uses it. With "HTTP bağlantısına izin ver" (Allow HTTP connection) on, catalogue, stream and sign-in details may travel unencrypted.

TMDB: film and series information

  • What is sent: the names and years of films and series in your catalogue (to recognise them), the TMDB ID of the title you open, language and region. For recommendations, genres derived from what you watched and favourited, and the ID of the series you watched last. No account or device identifier is sent.
  • Why: for artwork, overviews, cast, ratings, trailers, recommendations and streaming-platform information.
  • How: requests go from your device to TMDB. If the app's TMDB key cannot be used, the same requests are relayed through LeTa's server (see "LeTa's server" below). Artwork loads from TMDB's image server.
  • Your choice: under Ayarlar › Kitaplık ve Profiller › Film ve Dizi › TMDB ve içerik bilgileri › Otomatik bilgi indirme (Settings › Library and Profiles › Films and Series › TMDB and content information › Automatic information download) you can choose "Yalnız Wi-Fi" (Wi-Fi only) or "Kapalı" (Off).

JustWatch: streaming-platform information

Where a title can be watched is JustWatch data and arrives in TMDB's answer. LeTa does not connect to JustWatch separately.

YouTube: trailers

When you press the trailer button, the trailer TMDB provides opens in the YouTube app or on YouTube's website. LeTa does not embed the trailer and sends nothing to YouTube itself. Once you open the trailer, YouTube's (Google's) terms and privacy policy apply.

Trakt

  • Without connecting an account: for Trakt's public lists (trending, anticipated) and community ratings, the ID of the title you open and LeTa's Trakt app ID are sent. Nothing about you or your profile is sent.
  • If you connect your account (LeTa Pro): the connection uses a device code: LeTa shows a code and you confirm it on Trakt's page; LeTa never sees your Trakt password. The session is kept in the Keychain. Your watched marks, resume points, watchlist and ratings come from Trakt. Sent to Trakt: with playback scrobbling on, the ID, progress and playback state of the title you watch; with sending on, your watched marks; watchlist and rating changes only when you choose them. Your catalogue, favourites, channel list and source addresses are never sent.
  • Your choice: you can turn Trakt off under Ayarlar › Sistem ve Yardım › Veri kaynakları ve atıflar (Settings › System and Help › Data sources and credits). Disconnecting removes the session from this device; you can also remove LeTa's access in the settings on Trakt's website.

TVmaze

  • What is sent: the IMDb or TheTVDB ID of a series whose episode details are missing on TMDB. For the digital series premiere calendar, only the country code (Turkey) and the day; which series are in your catalogue is compared on your device.
  • Why: for missing season and episode details and premiere dates.
  • Your choice: you can turn it off under Veri kaynakları ve atıflar (Data sources and credits).

Wikidata

  • What is sent: the TMDB ID of the title you open. Requests carry LeTa's name and web address as identification.
  • Why: to find other IDs, such as IMDb and TheTVDB.
  • Your choice: you can turn it off under Veri kaynakları ve atıflar (Data sources and credits).

Apple

The App Store and purchases, Sign in with Apple, Siri, speech recognition, App Attest, CarPlay, AirPlay and notifications are Apple's services; Apple's privacy policy applies to them: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/

  • Voice search (iPhone and iPad): listening starts when you press the microphone button on the Ara (Search) page. If the device supports it, recognition happens on the device and your voice does not leave it; otherwise the audio goes to Apple's speech recognition service. To recognise names correctly, up to 50 of your recent searches and favourite channel names are given as hints. LeTa does not record your voice.
  • Siri: Apple processes what you say to Siri. LeTa receives only the channel, film or series name Siri passes on and looks it up in the catalogue on your device.

Google

  • Sign in with Google (when offered, iPhone and iPad): you sign in on Google's page, in a browser window that shares no cookies. Google shares the e-mail address and name you allow with LeTa.
  • iPhone browser: if you type search text instead of a web address into the address field, the text is sent to Google Search.
  • Google's privacy policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

LeTa's server

The server LeTa runs is hosted on Supabase in the European Union (Ireland). It is used for the optional LeTa account, LeTa Pro, sync, signing in on Apple TV with your phone, and relaying some information-service requests. The account and sync are described separately below.

  • When it relays information-service requests, the server does not link a request to a person and does not record its content. It keeps only answers that are the same for everyone (for example a film's details) in a shared cache for a short time. The number of requests is limited to prevent abuse.
  • To confirm that these requests come from a genuine LeTa installation, Apple's App Attest service is used: the device creates a key for this installation; the server keeps only the public part of the key and a counter, not who you are or details about your device.
  • The hosting service's technical logs may keep the IP address and time of a request for a short time.

LeTa account and sync

The LeTa account is optional. It lets you use the same Pro on your other devices, manage your devices and move your data between them. You can sign in with Apple, with Google (when offered) or with an e-mail address and password. On Apple TV you can also sign in with your phone, using a code or a QR code.

Kept with the account (LeTa's server, European Union, Ireland):

  • your e-mail address; with Sign in with Apple, the address Apple gives (it may be Apple's private relay address) and the account identifier; with Google, the e-mail address and name Google shares;
  • the name you choose to share;
  • with e-mail sign-in, a one-way hash of your password (the password itself is not stored);
  • for each device on your account, a random identifier made for the installation, the device's name, type, platform, app version and last activity time;
  • your Pro status (see "LeTa Pro and purchases").

Confirmation and password-reset e-mails are sent through the e-mail system of the hosting service (Supabase).

Signing in on Apple TV with your phone: the name the TV reports and the sign-in code are stored. The code is valid for 10 minutes; the record is deleted about a day after it expires. To limit abuse, a one-way hash of the network address the request came from is kept.

Pro cloud sync (only while it is on): favourites, playback positions, history, the "İzlendi" (Watched) and "Devam listesinden kaldır" (Remove from Continue Watching) marks you set, profiles (including the LeTa avatar; profile photos stay on the device), İzleme Listem (My Watchlist), collections, the order of favourites and "Sıradaki" (Up Next), hidden and locked categories, hidden titles and channels, channel and category order, guide matches and channel time corrections, matches and names you gave titles, theme, navigation and page layout, player, catalogue and guide preferences, the names, settings and order of your sources, and the limits the main profile set are stored on the server so that the devices on your account share them. Your sources' addresses, user names and passwords are not part of this sync. Channel lists and guides are not uploaded; each device loads them from its own source. Downloads stay on the device.

These records are protected on the server by access control; they are not additionally encrypted on your device. LeTa's server can read them.

"Kaynaklarımı hesabımda sakla" (Keep my sources in my account; off by default): if you turn it on, the address, user name, password and required connection headers of your M3U and Xtream sources are sent to the server and stored there encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is on LeTa's server; the server opens these details only for requests from your account and offers to add the source on your other devices. "Hesaptaki kaynak kayıtlarını sil" (Delete source records in the account) removes them all. With the option off, your sources' addresses and sign-in details do not leave your device.

Your own sync server: if you connect your own LeTa sync server or your own Supabase project, records go to the server you choose and are encrypted on your device with a key derived from your recovery phrase.

Deleting the account: Ayarlar › Hesap, Eşitleme ve Yedek › Cihazlar ve Senkronizasyon › LeTa hesabı ve Pro › Hesabı sil (Settings › Account, Sync and Backup › Devices and Sync › LeTa account and Pro › Delete account). Deletion removes the account details, the device list, the cloud records, the sources stored in the account, Apple TV sign-in codes and the customer record at RevenueCat (if RevenueCat does not answer at that moment, the server completes the deletion shortly after). Purchase event records may be kept with their link to the account removed. The App Store subscription is not cancelled with the account; see "LeTa Pro and purchases" to cancel. Signing out or removing a device from your account only ends that device's session; it does not delete the sources, favourites or media files on it.

LeTa Pro and purchases

LeTa Pro is an auto-renewable subscription bought through the App Store. Apple takes the payment; your card and payment details never reach LeTa or RevenueCat.

RevenueCat is used to verify the subscription. When LeTa opens, and during a purchase or restore, RevenueCat receives:

  • your LeTa account ID (a random identifier, not your e-mail) or, without an account, a random identifier RevenueCat creates;
  • App Store purchase details: the product, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, trial status and the store;
  • technical details RevenueCat sends with its requests: Apple's device identifier specific to this app's developer (identifierForVendor), the device model, system and app version, your preferred languages, the App Store country and the IP address.

RevenueCat's own privacy policy is on its website: https://www.revenuecat.com

LeTa's server keeps the Pro status linked to your account (plan, product, store, expiry date, whether it will renew, last verification time) and a log of purchase events (event type, product, store, date). The server reads the Pro status again from RevenueCat; it does not rely on the app's own claim.

You manage and cancel the subscription in your Apple ID settings. Details are in the Terms of Use: https://letaworks.com/iptv/en/terms

Nearby devices, transfers and backups

  • Nearby devices: sync works between LeTa devices you paired on the same local network. Pairing uses a QR code and a 6-digit PIN. Transfers are encrypted with the pairing key and are not sent to an internet server. Progress, preferences and profile photos can travel this way; a source you send from your phone to Apple TV is added once the main profile on Apple TV approves it.
  • Sending a source to a device that is not paired: the QR code carries only the source's name, not its address or sign-in details. The code is valid for 10 minutes and closes after three wrong attempts.
  • Backups (iPhone and iPad): a backup file contains profiles, watch history, favourites, lists, preferences, recording plans and the source list with its connection details, and is encrypted with AES-GCM using the password you choose. PINs are not included. Automatic backups are written to Files › LeTa › LeTa Yedekleri and are also included in your device's backup (iCloud or computer); connection details of sources locked with the main profile's PIN are not included in automatic backups. A backup file you export or share is under your control. Apple TV does not create backup files.

Profiles, PINs and children

  • Profiles, PINs and the content limits of child profiles are applied on the device. Account, purchase and source actions are available only in the unlocked main profile; other profiles see only the status.
  • Profile photos: chosen with the system photo picker; LeTa sees only the photo you pick. The photo is processed on the device and stored for the profile; the position of a face may be evaluated on the device to adjust the crop. Photos are not sent to any face-recognition service.
  • Children: LeTa does not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child's information has reached us, write to contact@letaworks.com and we will delete the record.

CarPlay and the iPhone browser

  • CarPlay: audio plays with the system media controls. Video opens with the system player only in cars that support video, when the car and iOS allow it. CarPlay asks for no location, speed or motion access; LeTa does not look at driving or parking state, and the car and iOS decide when a picture may be shown. Whether the last connected car reported video support, and when it connected, is kept only on your device to show it on the CarPlay page.
  • iPhone browser (on the CarPlay page): sites you open may use their own cookies and session data. If you type text instead of a web address into the address field, it is sent to Google Search. If bookmark icons are on, they are downloaded from the sites over a connection without cookies. Files you download in the browser are kept only in LeTa's own folder. Clearing the browser's site data removes cookies and saved web sessions; bookmarks are kept separately. For your data on sites you visit, those sites' own rules apply.

Notifications, diagnostic records and remote settings

  • Notifications: with your permission, programme, recording and episode reminders are shown by your device's own notification system; LeTa uses no notification server for them. You can change the permission in the system settings. Apple TV does not show notification text.
  • Playback and app measurements: measurements made only of seconds and counts, such as stream start and channel-change times, stalls, counts of error types, launch and catalogue times, are kept on your device; they contain no content names, addresses, sources or account details.
  • Crash records: on iPhone and iPad, Apple's MetricKit service gives the app reports on crashes, hangs, excessive resource use and slow launches. LeTa keeps their type, time, app and system version, error name and the first lines of the call stack on the device, at most 40 records. Apple TV does not provide these reports; there LeTa only records that the app closed while a player was open. Apple's own analytics sharing depends on your device settings; LeTa does not change it.
  • Sharing only by your choice: no diagnostic record is sent automatically. "Tanı paketini paylaş" (Share diagnostic package) prepares a file with the app and system version, device model, network type and VPN state, the number of source types, measurements, crash records, the last stream diagnosis and recent events. Addresses, server names, user names, passwords, e-mail and IP addresses, and source, profile and device names are removed from the package; channel, film and series names are removed too. The package goes only where you choose: on iPhone and iPad to the destination you pick in the share sheet, on Apple TV to the iPhone or iPad you paired or to your phone through the QR code on screen. With "Sorun bildir" (Report a problem), the note you write, the page you pick, the app and system version and the device model are added to an e-mail; if "Tanı paketini ekle" (Attach diagnostic package) is on, the package is attached too. On Apple TV the QR code opens these details as a ready e-mail on your phone; the package goes to a paired iPhone or iPad only if you choose so. In the log shared with "Günlüğü metin olarak paylaş" (Share log as text), credentials are removed, but source or profile names and error details may be included; you can check it before sharing.
  • Deleting: you can delete measurements, crash records and the log under Ayarlar › Sistem ve Yardım › Depolama ve Bakım › Tanı günlüğü › Tanı kayıtlarını sil (Settings › System and Help › Storage and Maintenance › Diagnostic log › Delete diagnostic records).
  • Remote settings: LeTa is built so that it can download a small signed settings file, to switch off a player or feature that causes problems without waiting for a new version. In this version the file's address is not set, so no such request is made. If an address is set, the request carries no account, device identifier or tracking information; the server sees only the IP address and the time. For staged rules the app generates a random number between 0 and 99 and keeps it only on the device.

Ads, tracking and analytics

LeTa shows no ads, does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA) and does not ask for permission to track you across apps. The app contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising service. The technical details RevenueCat sends to verify subscriptions are listed under "LeTa Pro and purchases".

TestFlight test version

This section applies only to the test version installed from TestFlight. The services below are off in the App Store version; if they are turned on in the App Store version, this policy will be updated first.

  • Additional information services: Fanart.tv (title logos and artwork; content ID), MDBList (IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes, Metacritic and Letterboxd ratings; IMDb ID) and OpenSubtitles (subtitle search and download; content ID, language and your OpenSubtitles session). These requests are relayed through LeTa's server with App Attest; LeTa's service key is added on the server. When you sign in to OpenSubtitles, your user name and password are passed from the server to OpenSubtitles and are not stored on the server. If “Bu cihazda bağlı kal” (Stay signed in on this device) is on when you sign in (the default), your user name and password are kept only in this device's Keychain and are sent the same way, only to the OpenSubtitles sign-in, to reconnect you when the session runs out (about a day); they are deleted when you turn it off or sign out.
  • LeTa Spor match data (API-SPORTS): scores, minutes, events, line-ups and standings are fetched through LeTa's server. Requests contain only the requested day or the match, league, team or player number; your LeTa account, channel names and what you watch are not sent. Answers are kept in a shared cache for everyone for a short time. Club crests and player photos load directly from the provider's image server, which can see your IP address. The on-screen scoreboard is read on your device; the picture is not sent anywhere.
  • Pro features: in the test version, Pro features that run on the device are open whatever the plan; cloud sync and the device limit still follow the account's real plan.
  • Apple TestFlight: if you choose to send a crash report, a screenshot or written feedback, Apple passes it to the developer through TestFlight. This is Apple's service; LeTa itself sends nothing.

In the App Store version, score and fixture data are off. The Spor (Sports) page uses the sports channels and guide of the source you added; which channel shows a match is worked out on your device from the guide.

Retention and deletion

  • Information on your device stays until you delete it. You can delete sources in the source settings, favourites and history in the library, and downloads and recordings in their own lists. Under Ayarlar › Sistem ve Yardım › Depolama ve Bakım (Settings › System and Help › Storage and Maintenance) you can clear the image cache, the rewind buffer and diagnostic records. Resetting settings resets preferences only; it does not delete accounts, sources or media files.
  • Account and sync records on LeTa's server are kept as long as the account exists and are deleted when you delete it. Purchase event records may remain with their link to the account removed. Apple TV sign-in codes are deleted within about a day. Cached answers that are the same for everyone are refreshed when they expire.
  • If you never created a LeTa account, LeTa's server holds no account record in your name; RevenueCat may hold a customer record under a random identifier.
  • E-mails you send us are used only to answer you and to complete your request.

Security

  • Sensitive details are kept in the device Keychain, for this device only; the library database is encrypted.
  • Connections to LeTa's server and to information services are encrypted with HTTPS. The connection to your own sources depends on the source's address; sources with http:// addresses need your separate permission.
  • On the server each user can reach only their own records. Source details stored in the account are encrypted with AES-256-GCM; the key is kept in a separate vault.
  • Backups are encrypted with your password and nearby transfers with the pairing key; PINs are stored as one-way hashes.
  • No method is perfect. If you notice a security problem, write to contact@letaworks.com. If we learn that your personal data has been obtained by unauthorised people, we will inform you and the Personal Data Protection Board as Article 12 of the KVKK requires.

These web pages

These pages on letaworks.com use no cookies, tracking code, external fonts or external images. The site's hosting service may keep access logs (IP address, time, requested page) as part of normal server operation.

KVKK: information under Law No. 6698

Data controller

LeTa Works (see "Data controller and contact"). E-mail: contact@letaworks.com

Personal data processed

  • Identity and contact: e-mail address, the name you choose to share, Apple or Google account identifier.
  • Customer transactions: LeTa Pro status, purchase events, RevenueCat customer ID.
  • Transaction security: session details, a one-way hash of the password, device records (random identifier, name, type, platform, app version, last activity), the App Attest public key, a one-way hash of the network address, IP address.
  • Usage and preference records: if you turn on Pro cloud sync, the library records and preferences that are synced.
  • Source details: if you turn on "Kaynaklarımı hesabımda sakla" (Keep my sources in my account), source addresses and sign-in details.
  • Requests and correspondence: e-mails you send us.

Purposes and legal grounds

  • Creating and managing your LeTa account, managing sign-ins and devices, signing in on Apple TV with your phone: establishing and performing the contract (KVKK Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Pro cloud sync and "Kaynaklarımı hesabımda sakla" (only if you turn them on): performing the contract (Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Verifying your LeTa Pro subscription and linking it to your account: performing the contract (Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Keeping purchase event records and using them for disputes and refund questions: establishing, exercising or protecting a right (Art. 5(2)(e)) and legal obligations (Art. 5(2)(ç)).
  • Protecting the service against abuse (request limits, App Attest, the network-address hash): legitimate interest (Art. 5(2)(f)).
  • Fetching film and series information, ratings and trailers; syncing with Trakt if you connect it: providing the feature you ask for, that is, performing the contract (Art. 5(2)(c)).
  • Answering your questions and requests: performing the contract and legal obligations (Art. 5(2)(c) and (ç)).

How data is collected

Personal data is collected electronically and automatically through the app when you use a LeTa feature, and when you send us an e-mail.

Transfers

Personal data is transferred only for the purposes above, and only as far as needed, to these recipients:

  • Supabase: hosts LeTa's server; data is stored in the European Union (Ireland).
  • RevenueCat: verifies the LeTa Pro subscription.
  • Apple: App Store purchases, Sign in with Apple and App Attest; speech recognition when voice search cannot run on the device.
  • Google: only if you sign in with Google.
  • Information services: TMDB, Trakt, TVmaze, Wikidata and YouTube; from your device and with your IP address, only the information described in this policy. If you connect a Trakt account, your watch information also goes to Trakt.
  • Competent authorities: only where there is a legal obligation.

The servers of these services are outside Turkey; this use may count as a transfer of personal data abroad. Transfers abroad are subject to the rules of Article 9 of the KVKK. You choose the providers of the sources you add; information sent to them goes, at your request, to wherever the provider is.

Your rights under Article 11 of the KVKK

  • to learn whether your personal data is processed,
  • if it is, to request information about it,
  • to learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used for that purpose,
  • to know the third parties in Turkey or abroad to whom it is transferred,
  • to ask for it to be corrected if it is incomplete or wrong,
  • to ask for it to be deleted or destroyed under the conditions of Article 7 of the KVKK,
  • to ask that corrections, deletions or destruction be notified to the third parties the data was transferred to,
  • to object to a result against you that arises solely from analysis by automated systems,
  • to claim compensation if you suffer damage because of unlawful processing.

How to apply

  • If you have a LeTa account, it is enough to write to contact@letaworks.com from the e-mail address registered in your account. If you have no account or write from another address, you can also use the other channels in the Communiqué on the Procedures and Principles of Application to the Data Controller: a written application, registered electronic mail (KEP), a secure electronic signature or a mobile signature.
  • Under the Communiqué, an application states your name and surname, your Turkish ID number if you are a Turkish citizen (otherwise your nationality and passport or ID number), your residential or business address, an e-mail address or telephone number for notification if you have one, and your request. We may ask for further information to verify your identity.
  • You do not need to apply to delete your account: you can delete it in the app at once.
  • Applications are free in principle; if handling the request causes an additional cost, a fee may be charged under the tariff set by the Personal Data Protection Board. The law requires an application to be concluded within 30 days at the latest.
  • If your application is rejected, you find the answer insufficient or you get no answer in time, you can complain to the Personal Data Protection Board under Article 14 of the KVKK within 30 days of learning the answer and in any case within 60 days of your application: https://www.kvkk.gov.tr

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date. An important change, such as collecting a new kind of information or using information for a new purpose, will also be mentioned in the Yenilikler (What's New) note of the related app update.
